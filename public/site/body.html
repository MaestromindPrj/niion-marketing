The NIION 5-stage system

A complete revenue infrastructure

Brand + content + trust. Funnels + tracking. Ads + retargeting. Pipeline + follow-up. Automation + conversion.

01

Authority Engine

Build market trust through precision content and founder positioning. Make your brand the obvious choice before prospects ever talk to sales.

  • 10 videos per month: problem awareness, trust building and solution positioning.
  • LinkedIn: thought leadership that reaches B2B decision-makers.
  • Instagram: reach, engagement and brand recall.
  • Facebook: re-engage warm audiences and amplify your best content.
02

Conversion Infrastructure

Engineer the entire journey from click to qualified lead with purpose-built pages and tracking.

  • High-converting pages: copy, design, UX and A/B testing from day one.
  • Meta Pixel, GA4 and heat maps for full data visibility.
  • Retargeting audiences built from day one.
  • Visitor → landing page → lead capture → pixel fired → qualified lead.
03

Performance Marketing

Precision campaigns across every buyer stage build a predictable pipeline independent of referrals.

  • Awareness: broad targeting, problem-aware content and brand reach.
  • Consideration: warm audience nurturing and solution content.
  • Conversion: direct offers for high-intent audiences.
  • Retarget website visitors, 75%+ video viewers, and page or profile engagers.
04

CRM System

Your command centre for full pipeline visibility, automated follow-ups and lead management.

  • New lead → contacted → qualified → closed won.
  • A live dashboard for pipeline value and deal velocity.
  • Behaviour-triggered follow-ups that nurture every lead.
  • Ads and website enquiries flow directly into the CRM.
05

Automation Engine

Give every lead an instant, personalised response, then qualify and route them automatically.

  • Connect leads from ads, organic, referrals and your website.
  • Personalised responses within 90 seconds, around the clock.
  • Behaviour-based scoring and routing to the right team.
  • Designed for complete lead coverage and fewer missed opportunities.
